# Privacy

Nothing leaves this device.

## What Lectern does not do

- It makes **no network request** once installed: no analytics, no fonts or scripts from elsewhere.
- It never writes to your folder.
- It has no account and no server.

## What it keeps, on this device

The folders you opened (so it can open them again), your last document and scroll position, your theme, and a search index of your documents. All of it lives in your browser's storage for Lectern, and nowhere else.

## Documents it does not trust

Documentation can contain anything. Markdown is sanitized before display. HTML documents open in a sandbox without scripts; you can turn scripts on for one document, and they still cannot reach your files, Lectern's storage or the network.
